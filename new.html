How it worksSee an example

Paste your channel. Get your page.

We read your newest videos, keep only the ones where you're talking, and build a page that answers people with the exact moment you said it. It takes about ten minutes.

Free to build and try. If it's your channel, claim it to add your own link. Free during launch.

Previews are unofficial until the channel's owner claims them, and they're kept out of search engines.

The code is open source: github.com/cgallic/creator-intake
